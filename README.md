# 🧠 KCNQ3-Lens

**Quantitative EEG analysis for families of children with rare epilepsies.**

KCNQ3-Lens is an open-source tool that helps families and clinicians look at pediatric EEG recordings with the same quantitative lens used in research — without sending any data to the cloud, and without needing a research lab.

It was built because parents of children with KCNQ3-spectrum conditions (and other rare channelopathies) often receive EEG reports that say things like "multi-regional spikes, no clinical seizures" without the quantitative detail that modern epilepsy research considers essential: *where* exactly the spikes are, *when* during sleep they peak, what their *morphology* looks like, whether *sleep spindles* are reduced, and whether there are subclinical *sustained bursts* not flagged as seizures.

This tool computes those things and presents them in a form you can take to your doctor.

> ⚠️ **KCNQ3-Lens is not a medical device.** It does not diagnose, treat, or replace clinical EEG interpretation. See [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md) before use.

---

## What it does

Five quantitative analyses on a single EEG recording:

| Analysis | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Topography** | Per-channel kurtosis to map where epileptiform activity is concentrated |
| **Sleep spindles** | Density per minute on Cz (or other central channel), with age-normative comparison |
| **Background power** | Posterior dominant rhythm frequency, delta/alpha ratio, band breakdown |
| **Sustained bursts** | Detection of rhythmic bursts ≥3s that may represent subclinical electrographic events |
| **Spike morphology** | Classification: simple spike vs sharp wave vs complex spike-wave |

Each analysis returns numerical findings, plotted results, and (optionally) a plain-language interpretation generated by the AI provider of your choice, using your own API key.

**Supported AI providers** (pick one — bring your own key):
- **Claude** (Anthropic) — [get a key](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys)
- **GPT** (OpenAI) — [get a key](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys)
- **Gemini** (Google) — [get a key](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey)

## What this tool is for — and what it is not

### ✅ This tool is for

- Parents of children with rare epilepsies who want to quantitatively understand their child's EEG
- Tracking longitudinal change in EEG metrics across recordings (pre/post medication, before/after treatment changes)
- Generating structured findings and questions to bring to a child's neurologist
- KCNQ3-spectrum patients specifically — Nihon Kohden EEG-1200A support and variant-specific context
- Open-source / reproducible quantitative pipelines that families can run themselves

### ❌ This tool is NOT for

- Replacing clinical EEG interpretation by a qualified neurologist
- Adult EEG (algorithms and reference ranges are tuned for pediatric data)
- Diagnosis of any condition
- Acute / emergency / ICU situations
- Real-time / streaming EEG analysis
- General EEG viewing (use [EDFbrowser](https://www.teuniz.net/edfbrowser/) for that)
- Research-grade source localization or advanced statistics (use [MNE-Python](https://mne.tools) directly)

## Scope & minimum requirements

KCNQ3-Lens is designed for **multi-hour overnight EEG recordings** of children with rare epilepsies. The analyses assume specific recording characteristics — using the tool on data outside these ranges will produce unreliable results.

**Minimum recording requirements:**

| Requirement | Minimum | Recommended | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 30 minutes | 6+ hours including sleep | Several analyses require a sleep period |
| Sampling rate | 100 Hz | 200–500 Hz | <100 Hz breaks filtering; >500 Hz needed for future HFO support |
| EEG channels | 6 (frontal + central + posterior) | 19 (full 10-20 system) | Standard 10-20 channel names expected |
| Child age | 2–18 years | 3–12 years | Best normative coverage in this range |
| Format | EDF, BDF, BrainVision, EEGLAB, Nihon Kohden EEG-1200A | — | See above |

**The tool will not analyze well:**

- Pure wake EEGs <30 minutes (sleep window required for spindle, burst, and morphology analyses)
- Recordings with <6 EEG channels (topography becomes meaningless)
- Recordings sampled below 100 Hz (filtering and morphology constraints)
- Recordings dominated by motion/muscle artifact (auto-detection of this is planned for v0.3)
- Adult recordings (age-normative ranges are pediatric)

---

## Supported EEG formats

- **Nihon Kohden EEG-1200A** (`.eeg`) — the long-term overnight format used by Nihon Kohden EEG-2100/2200 systems. **This reader is novel** — MNE-Python and EDFbrowser do not read this format correctly.
- **EDF / EDF+** (`.edf`) — most common open format
- **BDF** (`.bdf`)
- **BrainVision** (`.vhdr`)
- **EEGLAB** (`.set`)

## Install from source (developers)

You need Python 3.10 or newer.

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USER/kcnq3-lens.git
cd kcnq3-lens

# Create a virtual environment (recommended)
python -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate           # Linux/Mac
# .venv\Scripts\activate            # Windows

# Install dependencies
pip install -r requirements.txt

# Launch the app
streamlit run app.py
```

The app opens in your browser at `http://localhost:8501`.

## How to use

1. **Open the app.** It runs locally — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
2. **Enter your child's age** in the sidebar (used for age-normative comparisons).
3. **Optionally enter the genetic variant** (e.g. "KCNQ3 p.Arg230His") — only used in AI interpretation.
4. **Set the wake and sleep time windows** for the recording.
5. **Upload the EEG file** OR provide a local path (for very large files).
6. **Click "Run all analyses."** Takes 1–5 minutes depending on recording length.
7. **Review the findings** across five tabs.
8. (Optional) **Pick your AI provider** (Claude, GPT, or Gemini) and paste your API key in the sidebar to get a plain-language interpretation. Only numerical metrics are sent — raw EEG never leaves your machine.
9. **Download the JSON findings and/or the interpretation** to share with your doctor.

---

## Privacy and safety

- **All EEG processing happens on your machine.** No raw EEG data ever leaves your computer.
- **The AI interpretation feature is optional.** If you choose to use it, only derived numerical metrics (e.g., "spindle density: 1.3/min") are sent to the Anthropic API using your own key.
- **The tool does not store anything between sessions.** Closing the browser tab clears the results.
- **It is not a medical device.** Always discuss findings with your child's treating neurologist before acting on them.

## How KCNQ3-Lens compares to other EEG tools

KCNQ3-Lens does not replace existing tools — it fills a specific gap: **family-accessible quantitative EEG with optional AI interpretation, including support for the Nihon Kohden EEG-1200A format that no other open-source tool reads properly.**

| Tool | What it does well | When to use it instead | We complement with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[MNE-Python](https://mne.tools)** | Scientific EEG library for Python — preprocessing, ICA, source localization, full research stack | Custom research analyses, source modeling, advanced stats | We use MNE internally for non-NK formats and standard signal processing |
| **[YASA](https://github.com/raphaelvallat/yasa)** | Validated ML-based sleep staging and spindle detection | Polysomnography research, validated sleep analysis | Planned integration in v0.3 — our heuristic spindle detection will fall back to YASA when available |
| **[EDFbrowser](https://www.teuniz.net/edfbrowser/)** | Free, fast EDF/BDF waveform viewer | Visually scrolling through raw waveforms, manual marker placement | Use EDFbrowser for visual review, KCNQ3-Lens for quantitative summary |
| **[Persyst](https://www.persyst.com/)** | Clinical EEG software (commercial, paid) — spike detection, sleep staging, qEEG reports | In-hospital clinical workflows where licensed | We're an open-source, family-accessible alternative — no replacement for hospital tooling |
| **[Brainstorm](https://neuroimage.usc.edu/brainstorm/)** / **EEGLAB** / **FieldTrip** | MATLAB neuroimaging suites with advanced source modeling | Research labs with MATLAB licenses doing source-level analysis | Different audience; we don't compete on source localization |
| **[Luna](https://zzz.bwh.harvard.edu/luna/)** | Harvard's command-line sleep EEG toolkit | Sleep researchers running large cohort analyses | Different audience; we focus on per-family use with a GUI |
| **[NeuroKit2](https://github.com/neuropsychology/NeuroKit)** | General biosignal analysis (EEG, ECG, EDA, etc.) | Cross-signal research | Different scope; we focus on pediatric EEG only |

**The unique combination KCNQ3-Lens provides:**

1. **Nihon Kohden EEG-1200A reader** — no other open-source tool reads this format correctly
2. **Family-facing UI** — almost nothing in the open-source space targets parents
3. **Multi-provider AI interpretation** — Claude / GPT / Gemini, user's own key
4. **Pre/post-treatment comparison** — directly built into the UI
5. **Multi-language support** — open-source EEG tooling is almost exclusively English
6. **KCNQ3-spectrum context** — variant-aware reference ranges and discussion

## What KCNQ3-Lens is good at (and where it falls short)

**Good at:**
- Quantifying patterns that clinical EEG reports often summarize qualitatively
- Producing the same numbers consistently across recordings, so changes over months/years can be tracked
- Surfacing subclinical patterns (long bursts, low spindle density, slow background) that aren't always flagged
- Generating a structured report and specific questions you can take to your doctor

**Falls short:**
- Cannot replace the spatial-temporal pattern recognition of an experienced epileptologist
- Sleep staging is currently window-based (planned integration with YASA in v0.3 will give validated stage classification)
- The Nihon Kohden EEG-1200A reader is reverse-engineered from a single recording family — other recordings in this format may need verification
- Age-normative ranges are from the literature and may not represent every child
- Detection thresholds are conservative; some events may be missed or over-counted

## For developers

```
kcnq3-lens/
├── app.py                       # Streamlit entrypoint
├── src/
│   ├── readers/                 # EEG format readers
│   │   ├── nihon_kohden.py     # EEG-1200A binary reader (novel)
│   │   ├── edf.py              # EDF/EDF+/BDF via MNE
│   │   └── auto_detect.py
│   ├── analyses/
│   │   ├── topography.py
│   │   ├── spindles.py
│   │   ├── background.py
│   │   ├── bursts.py
│   │   └── morphology.py
│   └── ai/
│       ├── base.py              # Abstract LLM provider
│       ├── prompt.py            # Shared system prompt + payload builder
│       ├── router.py            # Provider dispatcher
│       └── providers/           # Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini implementations
├── requirements.txt
├── DISCLAIMER.md
└── LICENSE                       # MIT
```

Each analysis is a pure function: it takes an `EEGRecording` and parameters, returns a dataclass. You can use the modules without the Streamlit frontend for batch processing or research.
